Typesetting at the Speed of Typing

A document is a program, and an edit is a small change to it. Knuth's TeX [3] runs that program from the first page each time; an incremental build [1, 2] keeps its results, and reruns only the part an edit reaches.

D = pn ∘ pn−1 ∘ ⋯ ∘ p1     (1)

An edit to pk invalidates the states after it only where they read what pk wrote. The cost of a rebuild is therefore at most a fresh build's, and on most edits one paragraph's.

[1] Self-Adjusting ComputationU. A. Acar, 2005 · PhD thesis, Carnegie Mellon University
