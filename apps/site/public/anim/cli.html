Typesetting at the Speed of Thought

Ammar Seliaman    Suhaib Fahmy
Abstract
We show that a TEX engine can follow a writer keystroke by keystroke. The page is typeset again after every edit, in milliseconds, for papers and for books.

1   Introduction

Every writer knows the rhythm: change a word, press Recompile, wait, look, and change the next word. The wait is short, but it is paid thousands of times, and each payment breaks the thought being written.

This paper asks what a typesetting system would look like if the page were simply always correct. Not eventually, not after a build, but on the next frame after every keystroke. Section 2 describes the method and Section 3 the results [1].
