\section{A sunflower}
Seeds placed at a fixed angle from each other pack into spirals only when
that angle is the golden angle (Figure~\ref{fig:sunflower}).

\begin{figure}[h]
\centering
\def\seedangle{222.5}% KNOB: degrees between seeds
\def\seeds{140}%   KNOB: how many seeds
\fbox{\seedline{\seedangle}{\seeds}}
\caption{\seeds{} seeds, each turned \seedangle{} degrees from the last.}
\label{fig:sunflower}
\end{figure}
